Ready-made parts

Insert tab > Parts. Every part uses real standard sizes, and every number can be changed later in Properties.

  • Screws and bolts: socket, button, countersunk, hex and set screws, M2 to M10, by length.
  • Nuts and washers, bearings (608 and more, flanged, linear), dowel pins, smooth rods, round magnets, hex standoffs (female-female, male-female) and a printed standoff.
  • Holes and pockets: screw holes (plain, countersunk or counterbored), nut traps, heat-set insert holes and threaded holes.
  • Custom threads: a threaded bolt, nut or rod of any size and pitch, with a clearance for printing.
  • Aluminum extrusion: T-slot and V-slot profiles (2020, 2040, 4040…), any length.

Holes

Hole parts are gray and marked as holes (a dashed icon in the Objects list). Put one where the hole goes, pick it together with the solid and Merge (or Subtract): it is cut out. Tip: drag a screw over a hole and it snaps onto the middle of the hole. For a hole on a face without placing anything, see the Faces menu.

My parts

Keep your own parts for any project: pick an object and click Save to My parts (in Properties), or Parts > My parts > Save selected object. Every step that made it is kept, so it stays editable. Insert it again from Parts > My parts; Manage My parts… removes ones you no longer need. On a 3DJames box your parts are kept on the box and follow you to any computer.
