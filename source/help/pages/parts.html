Ready-made parts

Insert tab > Parts. Every part uses real standard sizes, and every number can be changed later in Properties, like any shape. Parts are made as solids, so you can merge them, round their edges and save them like anything else.

What is in the menu

  • Screws and bolts, Nuts and washers, Bearings, Pins, rods and magnets, Standoffs, Holes and pockets (screw holes, nut traps, heat-set insert holes, threaded holes), Custom threads and Aluminum extrusion (T-slot and V-slot, 2020 to 4040, and 2060 / 2080 any length).
  • Organizing: Gridfinity baseplates and bins, pegboard and IKEA Skadis hooks, drawer dividers, and boxes with a press-fit, sliding or hinged lid.
  • Electronics: standoffs and mounting holes for the Raspberry Pi, Pico and Arduino boards (or any four holes), fan mount plates and fan holes from 25 to 140 mm.
  • Joints: print-in-place and pin hinges, dovetails, pegs and sockets, a ball joint and a universal joint that print in place, shaft holes (D, double D, key slot), screw bosses and ribs.
  • Wall mounting and cables: keyhole slots, screw tabs, French cleats, cable clips, zip-tie anchors, cable-chain links, desk grommets.
  • Knobs, handles and feet: knurled and scalloped knobs, thumbscrews, drawer pulls, rubber-foot pockets, leveling feet.
  • Filament and printer: PTFE push-fit holes, filament guides, spool axles, bearing mounts and pockets, a snap-fit clip, a tolerance test, and a print-in-place hinge.
  • Motion and gears: GT2 pulleys, shaft collars, V-slot wheels, and gears (spur, helical, bevel), racks and worms.
  • Profiles, brackets and fasteners: square tube, I-beam, C-channel, angle and T profiles to any length, corner brackets, T-nuts, wood and sheet-metal screws, pop rivets.

What each one is for, and its numbers, are on Printer and workshop parts.

Holes

Hole parts are gray and marked as holes (a dashed icon in the Objects list); the Parts menu says "(to cut)" next to them. Put one where the hole goes, pick it together with the solid and Merge (or Subtract): it is cut out. Tip: drag a screw over a hole and it snaps onto the middle of the hole. For a hole on a face without placing anything, see the Faces menu.

Changing a part

Pick it and change its numbers in Properties (the slider next to a number shows the change as you drag). Where a part has a size to pick (a fan, a bearing, a screw), choosing another size fills in that size's standard numbers; you can still change any of them after. Sizing a part with the arrows in the view, or the bottom bar, stretches it as it is: change its numbers when you want an exact new size.

My parts

Keep your own parts for any project: pick an object and click Save to My parts (in Properties), or Parts > My parts > Save selected object. Every step that made it is kept, so it stays editable. Insert it again from Parts > My parts; Manage My parts… removes ones you no longer need. On a 3DJames box your parts are kept on the box and follow you to any computer.
