Pick, arrange and color

Once there are a few objects: pick several at once, line them up, keep them together, copy them, give them colors and names.

Pick more than one

  • Hold Ctrl and click objects to add them (or take them out again). On a tablet, switch on Pick more and tap.
  • Edit > Select all / Select none. Esc also picks nothing.
  • The order counts for some tools: the first object you pick is the one the others line up with (Align, Center) or are cut from.

Line up: Align and Center

  • Align (Object tab): pick two or more objects, click Align, and dots appear around them. Click a dot to line them all up on that side or on the middle. The first object you picked stays put; click another picked object to make it the one that stays.
  • Center (Object tab): click the object to center on, Ctrl+click the ones to move, then choose Center on it (left-right and front-back), in every direction, or one direction only.
  • Settle (or D) puts the picked objects down on the floor.

Keep together: Group

Group (Ctrl+G) keeps objects separate but moves and sizes them together: click any of them to pick the whole group. Ungroup (Ctrl+Shift+G) lets them go. To make them one solid instead, use Merge.

Copy, paste, duplicate, delete

  • Duplicate (Ctrl+D, Object or Edit tab): a copy beside the original.
  • Copy and Paste (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V): Paste adds a copy of what you copied, in this project. (To use an object in other projects, save it to My parts.)
  • Delete (Delete key) removes the picked objects.
  • Mirror images, turned copies and rows or circles of copies: Mirror, copies and patterns.

Colors

Pick objects and click a color on the Paint tab (or in Properties under Color). More opens a color picker for any color. Colors are saved in the project and go into 3MF files.

Names and the Objects list

The Objects tab of the side panel lists every object with its color and size. Click one to pick it (Ctrl+click for more). The eye hides an object from view without deleting it; click it again to show it.

  • To rename an object, click its name at the top of Properties and type.
  • Gray objects with a dashed icon are holes (screw holes, nut traps and other hole parts): merged with a solid, they are cut out of it.
