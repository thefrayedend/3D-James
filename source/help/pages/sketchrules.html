Sketch rules: keep your drawing right

A rule keeps part of your drawing the way you want it, like "this line stays level" or "these two lines stay the same length". Rules keep holding when you change things later. Change one size and everything else follows the rules.

Making a rule

  1. Click Select, then click the line, point or circle. To pick more than one, hold Shift while you click.
  2. Click a rule in the Rules row (under the drawing tools).

You can also do it the other way round: click the rule first, then click the things it is for. The bottom of the board tells you what to click next. Press Esc to stop.

Rule buttons that don't fit what you picked turn pale. Hover over any rule button to see what it does.

Example: draw a rectangle and a circle inside it. Pick the rectangle's bottom-left corner, Shift+click the circle's center, click Size, choose ↔ left-right and type 10. Do the same with ↕ up-down. The hole now stays 10 mm in from that corner, however you change the rectangle.

The rules

  • Flat: a line stays level (left-right). Pick two points instead and they stay at the same height.
  • Upright: a line stays straight up and down. With two points: one stays right above the other.
  • Parallel: two lines keep pointing the same way.
  • Square: two lines stay at a right angle (90°).
  • Same size: two lines stay the same length, or two circles (or arcs) stay the same size.
  • Touch: a line just touches a circle, or two circles just touch. Pick a corner where an arc meets a line and they join smoothly, without a bump.
  • Join: two points stay together, or a point stays on a line or circle.
  • Mirror pair: two points stay mirror images of each other. You choose the green line, the red line, or a line you pick.
  • Angle: type the angle of a line (from level), or the angle between two lines.
  • Size: type the length of a line, the size of a circle or arc, or the distance between two points. It stays that size. (Clicking a number on the drawing does the same.)
  • Freeze: a whole shape stays exactly where it is. Rules and dragging can't move it. Use Freeze again to unfreeze.

Seeing your rules

  • Every rule shows a small badge on the drawing. Hover over it to see what it is, click it to remove it.
  • All rules are also listed on the right under Rules, each with a ✕ to remove it.
  • If two rules fight each other (like a line that is both Flat and Upright), you get a message. Remove one of them, or press Undo.

The starting point is locked

The first point you click (the ⚓ anchor) stays where it is. Rules move the other points, never this one, so your drawing can't slide or wander off, for example past the green line. You can still drag it yourself.

To let rules move it too: click Unlock next to it in the Rules list, or click the ⚓ badge.

Green means locked

  • Lines and points turn green when rules and sizes hold them completely. Nothing can change them by accident.
  • Blue lines can still change when you drag or change something.
  • The note in the top-left corner says how many more sizes or rules it takes until everything is locked. When it says ✓ Fully locked, you're done.
  • You don't have to lock everything. It's just a help, so you know nothing will move by accident.

Locked sizes and "just showing" sizes

A size you typed holds (🔒). In the Rules list, click the 🔒 to make it just show the size (👁). The number stays on the drawing and follows along when things change, but it no longer holds anything. Click it again to lock it.

Rules you get without asking

  • Lines drawn level or upright, and rectangles, get Flat and Upright rules (see Level and upright, kept).
  • Slots keep their sides Parallel and their round ends smooth.
  • Helper lines snapped onto a corner or a line stay attached (a small purple dot): move that corner or line, and the helper line goes along.
  • Typing a size on the drawing, or with Measure, adds a locked size.

They are ordinary rules: they show as badges and in the Rules list, and you can remove any of them.
