Tutorial: four-hole mounting plate

You will learn: nested sketch outlines, exact circle centers, extrusion and editing an existing sketch. Finished size: 60 × 30 × 4 mm, with four 6 mm holes.

1. Draw the outside

For a fresh build, save your current project, choose File → New, then Insert → Sketch → On the floor. Draw a rectangle. Switch to Select, choose its outline, and enter these corner coordinates in the Shapes panel: (−30, −15), (30, −15), (30, 15), (−30, 15). Keep their order around the perimeter.

2. Add the holes

Draw four circles inside it. In the Shapes panel set each Diameter to 6 and use these Center values:

3. Make it solid

Choose Done → Push up, Height 4 mm, Result New object, then OK. Inspect the holes by orbiting. The inner circles are already subtracted; you do not need four separate cylinder cutters.

4. Edit and save

In History open Plate outline and four holes in the example. Change Height from 4 to 6. The same outline becomes thicker while the holes remain open. Edit sketch lets you change circle centers or diameters. Use Undo to compare, then Save as a .3dj project.

If a hole disappears, check that its circle is a closed outline inside the outside boundary. Cancel leaves the sketch without applying changes. A suppressed or failed earlier step can affect later operations.
