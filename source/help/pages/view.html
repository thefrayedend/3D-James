The view and settings

How you look at your model, and the settings that change how the app behaves. Everything here is on the View tab; your choices are remembered.

Looking around

  • Left-drag on empty space turns the view, right-drag (or middle-drag) slides it, the wheel zooms toward the pointer.
  • Home goes back to the start view; Fit (or F) fits everything in view, or just the picked objects.
  • Top, Front, Left, Right, Back, Bottom: look straight at the model from that side.
  • Look at face: click a face, and the view turns to look straight at it.
  • Double-click an object to fly in to it (again: closer; a third time: back out).

How objects are drawn

  • The Solid menu: Solid, Wireframe (just the lines), See-through, or X-ray (hidden edges show through), to see inside parts and holes.
  • Edges: dark lines along the edges of objects, on or off.
  • Reflection: the faint mirror picture of your objects in the floor, on or off. Turn it off for a plainer view, or when the reflection gets in the way of seeing what is under a part.
  • Flat view: no perspective, so sizes don't shrink with distance (like a technical drawing). Good with Top and Front.
  • Printer area: shows your printer's bed on the floor (Bambu Lab, Prusa, Creality, Voron and more), so you can see whether the model fits.
  • Theme: light, dark, or match Windows.

Snapping and measuring

  • Snap: how far a drag or a size change jumps (off, 0.1 to 10 mm).
  • Turn: the steps for turning (free, 5°, 15°, 45°, 90°).
  • Measure and Ruler: see Measure and Ruler.

Panels and touch

  • History and Objects open those tabs of the side panel (see History, variables and versions and Pick, arrange and color).
  • Touch: touch mode (bigger buttons, on-screen keys) switches on by itself when you use a finger or a pen; here you can keep it always on or always off. See Tablets and Apple Pencil.
  • Narrow window: buttons that don't fit go into More at the end of each row, with their labels.
