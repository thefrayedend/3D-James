Paint parts

Give parts of an object other colors: the top of a box, a stripe, a band round a cup, a logo's letters. It works like 3D Builder's Color tool, with the brushes of OrcaSlicer. Saved as a 3MF, the paint goes to OrcaSlicer and Bambu Studio as their own filament paint, so each color prints with its own filament (and you can still change the paint there).

Start and finish

  1. On the Paint tab, click Paint parts. The row of buttons changes to the paint tools.
  2. Click a color (More for any color), then a tool.
  3. Click or drag on an object to paint it. Drag beside the object to turn the view (the right mouse button and the wheel work as always).
  4. Click Done (or press Esc or Enter) to keep the paint: all of it is one step, so one Undo takes it off again. Cancel drops everything painted since you clicked Paint parts.

While painting, Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y undo and redo one stroke at a time.

The tools

  • Fill: point at a face and it lights up in the color; click to fill it, or keep the button down and drag across the object to fill every face you pass (like 3D Builder). Coverage says how far it spreads: over edges that bend less than that many degrees. At 0° it fills just that face; at 30° the whole round side of a cylinder; at 180° everything that touches. (Ctrl + mouse wheel changes it.)
  • Brush: a round brush on the surface you see (OrcaSlicer's Circle). The ring shows its size.
  • Sphere: paints everything within a ball around the pointer, round corners and onto every side it touches (OrcaSlicer's Sphere).
  • Band: click once to paint a level band all the way round the object, at that height, as tall as Size (OrcaSlicer's Height range). Good for stripes and color changes by height.
  • Eraser: rubs paint off, back to the object's own color. Holding Shift while you paint does the same with any tool.
  • Size: how wide the brush, sphere or band is, in mm. Alt + mouse wheel changes it while you paint.
  • Clear: takes all paint off the picked objects (or off every object, when none is picked).

The colors already used in the project are added at the end of the color list, so the same color is easy to find again. The Whole object colors next to Paint parts still color a whole object at once; paint goes on top of that color.

Changing the object afterwards

  • The paint goes along when you move, turn, size, duplicate or mirror the object, or change its position in Properties.
  • Merging objects keeps the paint of each of them on the merged object.
  • When you change a shape's own sizes (its width, a hole, a fillet), a Fill finds its face again and fills the whole face, however big it is now. Brush strokes, spheres and bands stay where you painted them, so a stroke can end up off the edge of a face that moved.

Into the slicer

Save as 3MF (Save as, then pick 3MF). In OrcaSlicer and Bambu Studio each color is on a filament of its own: first the objects' own colors, then the paint colors. Those programs keep the filament colors you have set, so after saving 3DJames shows which filament number each color is on: set the filaments to those colors (or let the AMS fill them in). To change the paint there, pick the object and use their Color painting tool.

STL and STEP files have no colors, so the paint is not in them. Holes cannot be painted (they are not printed).
