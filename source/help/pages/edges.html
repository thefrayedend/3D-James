Edges, faces, split and emboss

Finish a solid: round or bevel its edges, hollow it out, add a pocket or a boss on a face, cut it in two, or put text and logos on it. All on the Object tab. These tools need a solid (shapes, sketches, parts, STEP imports): imported STL, OBJ and 3MF meshes have no faces or edges to work on, so the buttons stay pale for them.

Round and bevel edges

Pick one object and click Round edges (fillet) or Bevel edges (chamfer). Click the edges to change, or use All, Top, Bottom or Vertical, and drag the slider: you see the result as you drag. Later, the step in History changes the radius or size. To pick different edges, delete the step and run the tool again.

Faces

The Faces menu (Object tab) works on one picked solid. Click the face when asked.

  • Hollow: makes a shell with a wall thickness. Click faces to leave them open (the top of a box, for example); with none clicked it is closed inside.
  • Shape on a face: a rectangle, circle, slot or polygon grown out of the face, or cut into it as a pocket or hole. It starts in the middle of the face you clicked; set its size, depth and Shift across / Shift up in Properties. For any other outline, sketch on the face.
  • Push/pull a face: click a flat face and type how far to move it. A positive distance pulls it out (adds material), a negative one pushes it in (makes a part shorter or a hole deeper).
  • Lay flat on a face: click a face, and the object turns so that face lies on the floor (the side you want on the print bed).
  • Heat-set insert pocket on a face: click a face for a ready-sized hole for a brass insert.

Split

Pick one object and click Split to cut it in two with a flat plane, for parts too big for the printer or to print in two halves. Move and tilt the plane in Properties (Move from center, Roll, Pitch, Yaw), and choose Keep top, Keep bottom or Keep both (two separate objects).

Emboss or engrave text and logos

Pick one solid, then Object > Emboss, and click a flat face. Type text (or choose a filled SVG logo), set its size and depth, and choose Raised or Recessed. Apply adds an editable step to History; Cancel leaves the model unchanged.

  • The artwork starts at the middle of the face. Shift across, Shift up and Turn place it. It is clipped where it runs off the face.
  • Logos: use an SVG made of filled shapes; convert text and strokes to paths in your drawing program first. Curves are followed closely, not exactly.
  • Flat faces only: emboss doesn't wrap around curved surfaces (a mug), and doesn't work on imported meshes.

A whole word standing on its own, not on a face: Insert > Text (see Add and place shapes). Worked example: the raised nameplate tutorial.
