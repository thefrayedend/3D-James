Combine objects

Join objects into one, cut one out of another, or keep only where they overlap. These are on the Object tab. Pick the objects first (hold Ctrl and click for more than one).

  • Merge: joins the picked objects into one solid. Holes (screw holes, nut traps and other hole parts) are cut out as it merges.
  • Subtract: pick the object to cut with (for example a cylinder for a hole) and click Subtract: it is cut out of everything it passes through. With several picked, the others are cut out of the first one you clicked.
  • Intersect: keeps only the part where the picked objects overlap.

With two or more objects picked, Properties shows the same three buttons in a Combine card.

One click, done

Merge, Subtract and Intersect happen as soon as you click them, like 3D Builder. Not what you wanted? Press Undo (Ctrl+Z): it is one Undo step. Or change it later in History (below).

Still changeable afterwards

A combine is a step in History, and the objects that went into it are kept inside it. Pick the result and open History to change the size or place of a hole you cut, or switch Merge to Subtract in the step's Operation setting. Delete the step to get the separate objects back.

Good to know

  • Objects that only touch at an edge or a point may not join. Let them overlap a little (0.1 mm is enough).
  • To keep objects together without joining them into one, use Group.
  • Imported meshes (STL, OBJ, 3MF) can be combined too, but very detailed meshes take longer.
