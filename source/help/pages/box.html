3DJames box (server)

A 3DJames box is a small computer (an Orange Pi Zero 4) on your home network that serves 3DJames to everyone in the house: PCs, Macs, tablets and phones. Each person signs in and gets their own folders for their designs. The box keeps the files, on its SD card or on your NAS. It is the same 3DJames app: only Open and Save are different.

Using it

  1. Open http://3djames.local in Edge, Chrome or Safari (or the address the person who set it up gave you).
  2. Sign in with your name and password.
  3. Draw as usual. Open and Save as show your folders on the box.
  • Save (Ctrl+S) saves back to the box. Your My parts are kept on the box too (in "3DJames Parts" in your folder), so they follow you to any computer.
  • Someone else has it open: when another person opens the same file, you both get a note. If they saved it after you opened it and you then save, 3DJames asks first: Save a copy (keeps both) or Replace their version.
  • Sign out: File menu (☰) > Sign out. Backups (File > Backups) are kept per person, so people sharing a tablet never see each other's work.
  • Export (STL, STEP, 3MF) can go to your folders on the box or be downloaded to your device.

Setting up a box (for the admin)

  1. Write the 3DJames box image to a micro-SD card (balenaEtcher or Raspberry Pi Imager). On a PC the card shows a small 3DJAMES drive: fill in your Wi-Fi in 3djames-config.txt (or use a network cable). READ-ME-FIRST.txt there has the details.
  2. Put the card in the board and plug in the power. After about 2 minutes open the admin page: http://3djames.local:8080
  3. Make the admin account (that's you).
  4. App tab: upload 3DJames.html (the same file you open on a PC).
  5. Users tab: add the people who will use it. Each gets a sign-in name, a password, and folders.

Where the files are kept

On the Storage tab: This box (its SD card) is always there. You can also keep everyone's designs on a NAS share (so they are backed up with everything else), or on a USB drive plugged into the box. Then, on the Users tab, pick for each person where their folders are (Edit > Add a folder > pick the place, and the folder inside it).

  • Example: if Windows shows the folder as \\192.168.1.166\Jon\3D Objects, the NAS address is 192.168.1.166 and the share name is Jon. "3D Objects" is the folder you pick later for a person.
  • A share that opens without a password: leave the NAS user name and password empty. Otherwise type the same name and password you use on your PC.
  • When you save, the box connects and saves (and deletes) a small test file, so you know straight away that it works. If not, it says what is wrong in plain words.
  • Works with nearly every NAS (Synology, QNAP, TrueNAS, a Windows PC sharing a folder). If the NAS is off for a while, the box connects again by itself when it is back. Folders on it can't be used meanwhile.
  • USB drives formatted by Windows or a Mac work as they are (exFAT, FAT32 or NTFS). Nothing on the drive is erased: 3DJames keeps its files in normal folders, so you can also plug the drive into a PC and see them.
  • Pulled the drive out? Plug it back in: it is ready again within a few seconds. Wait until a save has finished before you pull it out.
  • Shared folder tab: switch on a folder everyone you allow can use (tick "May use the shared folder" for each person). It can be read-only.

Updating 3DJames on the box

  • App tab > choose the new 3DJames.html > Upload and install. Everyone gets it the next time they open or reload 3DJames. No new SD card. Go back to returns to the version before.
  • Or switch on the Update folder there (with a password) and copy the new 3DJames.html to \\3djames\3DJames-update from any Windows PC (sign in as threedjames). It is installed within a few seconds; LAST-UPDATE.txt in that folder says how it went.

Good to know

  • The box only hands out 3DJames and keeps the files: the modelling itself runs on each person's computer or tablet, so the box never slows down.
  • It uses plain http, made for a home network. Don't open it to the internet.
  • No Wi-Fi details, or wrong ones? The box makes its own Wi-Fi called 3DJames-Setup-… (password 3djames123). Connect to it and open http://10.42.0.1:8080 to pick your Wi-Fi (Network & box tab).
  • Can't find the box? Its address is in MY-3DJAMES-ADDRESS.txt on the card's 3DJAMES drive (once it has been online), or in your router's list of devices.
