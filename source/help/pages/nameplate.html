Tutorial: raised nameplate

You will learn: exact primitive sizes, face picking, emboss settings and editable History. Finished base: 80 × 30 × 4 mm; raised lettering: 1 mm.

1. Size the base

Save your current project and start a new one. Insert a Cube. In Properties set Width 80, Depth 30 and Height 4 mm.

2. Add lettering

Select the base, choose Object → Emboss, and click its flat top face. Choose Text, enter MAKER, use DejaVu Sans Bold, Letter height 8 mm, Depth 1 mm, and Action Raised. Leave both shifts and the turn at zero to center the text. Choose Apply.

3. Make it yours

Open the example's Raised MAKER text History step and change the text to HELLO. Try Depth 1.5 mm, or switch to recessed engraving. Keep the lettering inside the base and use Undo to compare changes. Save the result as your own .3dj project.

For a logo, change Artwork to SVG and choose a filled SVG with text and strokes converted to outlines. Emboss currently needs a flat CAD face; it does not wrap lettering around a curved mug. Canceling the tool leaves the model unchanged.
