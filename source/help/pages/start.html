Getting started

3DJames is a free 3D design app for 3D printing. Everything runs on your own computer: nothing is sent online. This page shows the screen and the few things you need everywhere. The rest of Help follows the way you work: build with shapes, draw your own outlines (sketching), then check, save and print.

The screen

  • The File menu (☰) at the top left: New, Open, Save, Save as, versions and backups. Next to it are Save, Undo and Redo.
  • Tabs (Insert, Object, Edit, Paint, View, Help): each tab shows its own row of buttons underneath. In a narrow window, buttons that don't fit go into More at the end of the row.
  • The 3D view in the middle: your objects on an endless floor with millimetre rulers. The red line runs left-right (X), the green line front-back (Y); up is Z.
  • The side panel on the right, with three tabs: Properties (the exact sizes of what you picked), History (every step, changeable later) and Objects (a list of everything). The arrow at its top hides it; click the strip at the right edge to bring it back.
  • The bar at the bottom: Move, Turn and Size handles, and exact positions, turns or sizes for what is picked.

What is on each tab

InsertShapes (Cube, Cylinder, Sphere, Text…), Add a model from a file, Sketch (draw your own outline), Parts (screws, nuts, bearings, holes…) and Workplane
ObjectMerge, Subtract, Intersect, Emboss, Round and Bevel edges, Faces (Hollow, Push/pull…), Mirror, Pattern, Split, Group, Align, Center, Duplicate, Settle
EditUndo, Redo, Copy, Paste, Duplicate, Delete, Select all, Select none
PaintColors for the picked objects, and Paint parts: fill faces, brush, bands (see Paint parts)
ViewViews from each side, how objects are drawn, Measure, Ruler, snapping, printer area, theme and touch mode
HelpThis guide. Keep visible docks it beside your work

Looking around

  • Left-drag on empty space: turn the view. Right-drag: slide it. Mouse wheel: zoom. With nothing picked, Properties shows a short list of these.
  • Double-click an object: fly in so it fills the view. Again: twice as close. A third time: back to the first view.
  • F fits everything in view, Home goes back to the start view. More on the View tab.

Picking things

Click an object to pick it (it is outlined, and Properties shows it). Hold Ctrl and click to pick more than one. Click empty space to pick nothing. Most buttons work on what is picked, and stay pale until you pick something they can work on.

Your first part in a minute

  1. Insert > Cube. In Properties, set it to 40 × 40 × 10 mm.
  2. Drag the Cylinder button from the Insert tab onto the middle of the cube and let go (or click it, then drag the cylinder over the cube until the dotted purple lines show it is lined up with the cube's middle). Set its diameter to 8 and its height to 20.
  3. With the cylinder picked, Object > Subtract. The cube now has a hole.
  4. Pick the cube and open History: the cylinder's step is still there. Change its diameter to 10 and the hole follows.
  5. ☰ > Save keeps the editable project; ☰ > Save as also makes an STL, STEP or 3MF for your slicer.

Made a mistake?

Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Y redoes. Your work is also copied in the background: ☰ > Backups. Save a project file to keep it for good (see Saving and opening).
