Several sketches together

Change two or more sketch objects on one board: line them up with each other, join them into one sketch, or weld their outlines into one.

Edit them together

Hold Ctrl and click two or more sketch objects. In Properties, click Edit sketches together (also in Insert > Sketch > Edit picked sketches together). They open on one board, right where they are, each in its own color. The board shows real positions: the red and green lines cross at 0,0 of the floor, and sketches that are 20 mm apart in the model are 20 mm apart on the board. (A single Edit sketch shows the object at its real position too, also after you moved it.)

  • Change any outline the usual way. Shapes snap to the other sketch's corners and lines.
  • New shapes go here chooses which sketch the shapes you draw belong to. Pick a shape and click Move picked shape here to move it to another sketch.
  • Done: each sketch keeps its own height or spin and stays its own object. Cancel changes nothing.
  • Join into one sketch, then Done: all the shapes become part of the first sketch you picked (its height or spin is used), and the other sketch objects are removed. Outlines inside other outlines become holes, as usual. One Undo brings the separate sketches back.

Merge touching sketches into one outline

Join keeps every outline as it is. To weld outlines that touch or overlap into one outline, click Merge touching sketches (on the board, under Sketches). Outlines count as touching when they share a side or a corner, overlap, or are less than 0.01 mm apart (for example a rectangle drawn onto the end of an imported wall); they are welded with no seam lines left over. Outlines that don't touch anything stay exactly as they were. Arcs stay arcs and holes stay holes. A sketch whose shapes all got merged into another one is removed when you click Done. Undo (Ctrl+Z on the board) brings the separate outlines back.

In a single sketch, the same button is Merge touching outlines under Whole sketch. Shift+click two or more shapes first to merge only those. Rules on the merged outlines are removed (the new outline has new corners); other rules stay.

Good to know: the sketches must be drawn on the same flat plane: all on the floor (or on the same face, turned the same way), or all spun around the same line. Push up and Spin around sketches can't be mixed, and Sweeps or part turns open one at a time; Properties explains why when it can't open them together. Rules can only link shapes of the same sketch; a rule between two sketches is removed when you click Done (unless you joined them).
