Send feedback

Found something that doesn't work, or have an idea? Send feedback fills in a report for the people who make 3DJames. It goes on the 3DJames page on GitHub, where you can follow what happens to it.

  1. Click Send feedback at the bottom left of Help (or in the ☰ menu).
  2. Pick what it is about, give it a short title, and say what you did, what happened and what you expected. For an idea, say what you would like 3DJames to do.
  3. Leave Attach this project ticked when it is about a model: a copy of the project is saved to your Downloads folder, named like Box.3dj.zip.
  4. Click Open the report. Your browser opens a new report on GitHub with your words already in it, the 3DJames version, and any problems the model has (Also put in the report shows them first).
  5. Drag the .3dj.zip from your Downloads folder into the report's text box, look it all over, and click Create.
  • 3DJames itself sends nothing: you see the whole report before it goes, and you can change or remove anything in it.
  • GitHub needs a free account to post a report. Reports there can be read by anyone, so don't attach a project you want to keep private.
  • A .3dj.zip is the same as a .3dj project (GitHub only takes zip files): 3DJames opens it when you drop it on the window.
