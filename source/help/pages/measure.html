Measure and Ruler

Check sizes and distances on your model, and set exact gaps between objects. Neither changes the model or adds steps to History. (Measuring inside a drawing is done on the sketch board with its own Measure tool.)

Measure

View > Measure. Click two points on the surface of solids or meshes: the panel on the right shows the distance and how far apart they are in X, Y and Z. A third click starts a new measurement. Done removes the markers.

  • Switch to edges to get an edge's length, or the diameter or radius of a round edge.
  • Switch to faces to get a face's area, or the distance between two parallel flat faces (a wall's thickness).
  • Switch between millimetres and inches, and copy the numbers.
  • Edge lengths, round sizes and areas are worked out from the drawn model and are very close, not exact. A distance between two faces is between the points you clicked, not the shortest gap between curved surfaces.

Ruler: exact gaps

View > Ruler (on or off). Pick an object: the gaps around it are shown as numbers. Click a number and type the gap you want; the object moves to make it exactly that. Handy to put a part exactly 2 mm from another, or 5 mm in from an edge.

Sizes in Properties

Properties always shows the picked object's overall width × depth × height and its volume (in cm³: handy to guess print weight and filament). The bottom bar shows its position and, with the Size handles, its size.
