Printer and workshop parts

What the ready-made parts in Insert > Parts are for, and what their numbers mean. Each one is added with its standard numbers; change them in Properties. Parts marked (hole) are gray cutters: put one where the hole goes and Merge it (see Ready-made parts).

Parts that print in place

The hinge (the "Print-in-place" kind), ball joint and universal joint come off the printer already moving: each moving piece is a separate solid with a small gap round it (the clearance numbers). If a joint comes out stuck, raise the clearance by 0.1 mm; if it is sloppy, lower it. The tolerance test finds your printer's clearance in one print: a plate with holes that get 0.1 mm wider each time, and loose pegs. The smallest hole a peg turns freely in is the clearance to use.

Organizing

  • Gridfinity: its own page.
  • Wall hook: for pegboard (6.35 mm holes, 25.4 mm apart) or IKEA Skadis (5 × 15 mm slots). Reach, width, lip height and the peg or tab sizes can be changed. Turn it flat to print (Object > Faces > Lay flat). The Skadis dimensions are typical; check them against your board with a short test print.
  • Drawer divider: a flat piece with half-height slots, so two cross like an egg crate. Make one with the slots in the top edge and one with them in the bottom edge.
  • Box with lid: add the box and then the lid (the Part choice) with the same sizes. Press-fit: a rim on the lid fits inside the box. Sliding: the lid slides in from the front along grooves. Hinged: knuckles along the back; push a piece of 1.75 mm filament through as the pin.

Electronics

  • Board standoffs: posts at the mounting holes of a Raspberry Pi 5 / 4 / 3 / Zero, a Pico, an Arduino Uno or Mega, or any four holes (Any rectangle), on a base plate that covers the board. The hole in each post is for a self-tapping screw or a heat-set insert.
  • Board mounting holes (hole): just the holes, to cut through a plate or case.
  • Fan mount plate and Fan holes (hole): for 25, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70, 80, 92, 120 and 140 mm fans. Pick the size and the screw spacing, screw hole and airflow opening fill in.

Joints

  • Hinge: Print-in-place or a pin hinge where you add the pin (a nail or filament). Knuckles, leaf width and screw holes can be changed.
  • Dovetail tail and slot (hole): a sliding joint that cannot lift out. Make the slot a little wider than the tail (clearance).
  • Peg and peg socket (hole): for alignment pins between two printed halves.
  • Shaft hole (hole): D, double D, or round with a key slot, with clearance.
  • Screw boss and rib: a post with a pilot hole and gussets round it, and a triangular rib to stiffen a corner.

Wall mounting and cables

  • Keyhole slot (hole): hang a plate on a screw head: the big end lets the head in, the narrow slot holds the shank, and the pocket on the back leaves room for the head.
  • Screw tab: a mounting ear with a countersunk hole. French cleat: a matching pair of strips with a 45° slope; one goes on the wall, the other on what you hang.
  • Cable clip (4 to 15 mm cables), zip-tie anchor, cable-chain link (print several and join them with a pin), desk grommet (the hole size and the desk thickness are the numbers to set).

Knobs, handles and feet

  • Knob: knurled, scalloped or plain, with a shaft hole (round or D), an optional nut pocket underneath and a set-screw hole. Thumbscrew: wing, knurled or hex head on a plain shaft (give the shaft a thread with Custom threads, or print a hole and use a real bolt).
  • Drawer pull: two posts and a bar, with a screw hole up each post (hole centers 64, 96, 128 mm and others).
  • Foot pocket (hole): a recess for a rubber foot. Leveling foot: a pad with a stem.

Filament and printer

  • PTFE push-fit hole (hole): for PC4-M6, PC4-M10 and other pneumatic fittings, plus the tube hole behind it. Filament guide, spool axle (a rod with a flange and a cone the spool slides over).
  • Bearing mount: a block with a pocket for a bearing, on a base with screw holes. Bearing pocket (hole): the same pocket to cut into your own part. Pick the bearing (608, 625, 6000 and more).
  • Snap-fit clip: a flexible beam ending in a hook.

Motion

  • GT2 pulley for 6 mm belts, 10 to 80 teeth, with a hub and set screw, a round or D bore. Shaft collar with a set screw or a clamp. V-slot wheel, standard or mini, with seats for bearings.
  • Gears are on their own page.

Profiles, brackets and fasteners

  • Profile: square or rectangular tube, I-beam, C-channel, angle, T-beam and flat bar, to any length. Corner bracket with holes and a gusset. T-nut for 2020 and 3030 aluminium extrusion. The 3030 T-slot profile uses typical dimensions, so check it against your own extrusion.
  • Wood screws (countersunk or pan head) and sheet-metal screws, pop rivets: plain solids for fit checks and models; they have no thread detail.

Limits

Every part is a starting point built to common standard sizes, not to one maker's drawing. Check a fit with a quick print before you print a big one, and change the clearance numbers for your printer.
