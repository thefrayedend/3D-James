Tutorials: learn by making

Each lesson has a real, editable .3dj example. Open editable example opens a fresh copy in this app; Download .3dj saves it for later. If your current project has unsaved changes, save it first or choose explicitly to replace it.

Keep this guide beside your work: click Keep visible. Help stays docked on the left while you use Insert, Object and the other tabs, keeping the current topic and scroll position. Drag its bottom-right edge to resize it. Choose a different topic from its dropdown, expand with Full Help, or close with Close. This works while sketching, too. If you switch to another tab without clicking Keep visible, Help closes; click the Help tab to open it again where you left off.

Within an app session, Help remembers where you were. After closing and reopening the app, Help starts on About 3DJames. The model itself is saved separately.

Learn from the examples

Open History and choose a named step. Use Edit sketch to inspect outlines, change dimensions or tweak a corner. The examples contain real sketch and operation history, not a hidden finished STL. Save your experiments under your own project name; opening the bundled example again gives you the original.

Examples demonstrate modeling, not a tested engineering or manufacturing specification. In particular, the mug is a modeling exercise rather than a validated drinking vessel.
