Tablets and Apple Pencil

3DJames works with fingers and pens (an iPad with the Apple Pencil, an Android tablet, a touch-screen laptop) as well as with a mouse. Each one is handled its own way: using a finger never changes how the mouse works.

On an iPad, open 3DJames from a 3DJames box on your network (see 3DJames box (server)): a tablet can't easily open the 3DJames.html file itself.

Touch mode

As soon as you use a finger or a pen, 3DJames switches to touch mode: bigger buttons, and on-screen buttons for the keys a tablet doesn't have. Use a mouse again and it switches back. To keep it on or off: View tab > Touch > Automatic / Always on / Always off.

In the 3D view

Tap an objectPick it. Tap empty space to pick nothing
Pick more on, then tap objectsPick more than one (like Ctrl+click). Tap Pick more again to switch it off
Touch an object, hold still a moment, then dragMove it (like dragging it with the mouse). A short buzz says it is held, on phones that can
Swipe with one fingerTurn the view (also when the swipe starts on an object: only holding first moves it)
Pinch with two fingers / slide with two fingersZoom / slide the view
Double tap an objectFly in to it
Drag a shape button (Insert tab) down into the viewAdd the shape where you lift your finger (a see-through shape shows where)
The arrows and dots around a picked objectDrag them with a finger as with the mouse (the move arrows are on every side: use the closest)

In the sketch

A finger draws by touch, slide, lift: put your finger down, slide it until the point sits where you want it (it snaps to corners, loose ends and the grid like the mouse pointer), and lift your finger to place the point. So you can see where a point goes before it is placed. Two fingers slide and zoom the board; they never draw.

The keys stay on until you tap them again (they light up while on). Stop here and Close with a line on the right finish a line without a keyboard.

Trim with a finger: touch the line, slide until the piece you want gone is red, and lift. Rules, Offset, Pattern and the other buttons work by tapping, as with the mouse. Everything about the board itself: The sketch board.

Apple Pencil (and other pens)

  • The pen works like the mouse: exact, and points go where the tip touches (no slide-and-lift needed).
  • While you use the pen, your palm resting on the screen is ignored.
  • On iPads where the Pencil can hover above the screen, the snap marker and the line follow the Pencil before it touches.
  • Use fingers for the view (two fingers to slide and zoom) while you draw with the Pencil.

Good to know

  • Hover help (the little notes that appear when the mouse rests on a button) doesn't exist on a tablet. This Help has the same information.
  • Very big models are built on the tablet itself. Large STEP files can be too much for a tablet's memory: open those on a PC.
