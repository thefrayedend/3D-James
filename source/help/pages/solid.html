Make the sketch solid

Click Done on the sketch board and choose how the outline becomes solid. (Unfinished lines? Done asks about them first: see Unfinished lines.)

  • Push up: the outline gets a height (the usual choice: plates, brackets, lids).
  • Spin around: the outline spins around the green line, like a lathe (cups, knobs, wheels). See below.
  • Loft: goes from the first outline you drew (bottom) to the second (top), for example a square base to a round top. Draw exactly two outlines.
  • Sweep: carries the outline along a path. Draw the path with helper lines joined end to end (handles, pipes, rails).
  • Result: New object, or Cut a hole (cut out of whatever it passes through) / Cut into the face (when sketching on a face: as deep as the height). Cuts happen right away and stay editable in History.

Everything can be changed later: pick the object, and in Properties change the height, the way it is made solid, or click Edit sketch to change the outline. Several sketches can be changed on one board: see Several sketches together.

Spin around, step by step

The green line is what it spins around, so the whole outline must be on one side of it.

Click an empty spot, then Right of green line (under Whole sketch). The drawing slides over and touches the green line.

Click Done, pick Spin around, leave 360 and click OK.

Less than 360 makes part of a turn (a quarter ring at 90). Worked example: the mug tutorial spins a hollow cup from one outline.
