3DJames box - quick start
=========================

What you need
-------------
* This SD card (already prepared - you're reading it!)
* An Orange Pi Zero 4 and a USB-C power supply (5V / 3A or better)
* Your home network (a cable to the router, or Wi-Fi)

Step 1 - Wi-Fi  (skip if you plug the board into your router with a cable)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Open  3djames-config.txt  on this drive (double-click it, it opens in Notepad).
Fill in:
    wifi_name=YourWiFiName
    wifi_password=YourWiFiPassword
Save the file. Eject the SD card safely.

Step 2 - Start the box
----------------------
Put the SD card into the board and plug in the power.
The first start takes about 2 minutes (it sets itself up). Later starts are faster.

Step 3 - Set it up (admin page)
-------------------------------
On a computer, phone or tablet on the same network, open:

    http://3djames.local:8080

Make your admin account, then upload the 3DJames app (App tab: the 3DJames.html
file). Add the people who will use it (Users tab), and if you like, connect your
NAS (Storage tab) so everyone's designs are saved there.

Step 4 - Use 3DJames
--------------------
Everyone opens:

    http://3djames.local

signs in, and draws. Open and Save show their own folders (and the shared one).

Doesn't open?
 * Wait one more minute and try again.
 * Look in your router's app for a device called "3djames" and open its address,
   e.g. http://192.168.1.57 (and :8080 for the admin page).
 * Put the SD card back in your computer: the file MY-3DJAMES-ADDRESS.txt on it
   shows the box's address (it appears once the box was online).
 * Wi-Fi details wrong? The box then makes its own Wi-Fi network called
   "3DJames-Setup-XXXX" (password: 3djames123). Connect to it and open
   http://10.42.0.1:8080 - make the admin account if you haven't, then
   Network & box > Wi-Fi to pick your home Wi-Fi.

Updating 3DJames later
----------------------
Admin page > App > upload the new 3DJames.html. That's all: no new SD card.
Or switch on the Update folder there and copy the new file to
\\3djames\3DJames-update from any Windows PC.
