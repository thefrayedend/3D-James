3DJames help pictures
=====================
Finished guides (one picture each) are in this folder. Each guide has its own folder with:
  - the raw screenshots from the real app (s1.png, s2.png, ...) to reuse in the full help later
  - take_shots.py: drives the app and takes the screenshots (run through the test page)
  - make_guide.py: draws the red circles/arrows and puts the steps together

Guides:
  Spin-around-help.png   How to spin a sketch around the green line (version 0.8.5)
