VERSION 0.17.0 (Oct 20, 2026)
[x] Sketch tools (ideas from SolidCraft, kept simple; Help: More sketch tools):
    - Trim (T): click a piece of a line to cut it away up to the lines that cross it (shows red first). Overlapping
      shapes trim into one outline; circles and helper lines trim too.
    - Slot (O): two centers + width (or type length, width); stays a slot when dragged.
    - Polygon (P): center + corner, or type the size across flats (flat side at the bottom); 3 to 64 sides.
    - Offset: a copy bigger or smaller by a distance (walls, clearances); sharp or round corners; keep or replace.
    - Pattern: copies in a row (step x / y) or in a circle around the center of the sketch.
    - Make helper line / Make it a drawn line: switch straight sides between helper lines and the drawing.
    - Level & upright: lines drawn within 2 degrees of level / upright go exactly so and keep a Flat / Upright rule;
      rectangles keep 2 Flat + 2 Upright rules. Off under Snap to; Ctrl places freely.
    - Import DXF / SVG (Import on the board, or Insert > Sketch > From a DXF / SVG file): lines, arcs, circles,
      polylines with bulges, ellipses, splines, blocks; SVG paths and shapes. Lines whose ends meet join into outlines.
    Undo and Fit in the sketch top bar are icon-only now, so the bar fits on one row.
